# LinkedIn post (goes with out/ballet-l-ba7er-linkedin.mp4)

In Lebanon, when you want someone to go away, you tell them:
روح بلّط البحر, "go tile the sea."

Go do something pointless.

So I did. 🧱🌊

Ballet l Ba7er is a free 3D browser game where you tile the Mediterranean off the Saida corniche, one balata at a time, while the waves take your edges back.

What's in it:
→ Shovel mouna, push the wheelbarrow, lay tiles out to sea
→ Eat at Souk el Akel: a Soubras sandwich, falafel, bouza from el Samra
→ Take Abou Ali's boat out to Jazirat Ziri
→ Abou Nabil, Im Kamel, the Jardali and the rest of the regulars (don't hit Abou Nabil, he hits back)
→ Day turns to maghreb turns to night, and the sea never stops

Built with three.js, runs in any browser, phone or PC. No install, no sign-up. More coming soon.

Play: https://balletlba7er.com

Tell me how many metres you get before the sea wins.
